My Journey

A small library of where I've been.

Five chapters from school to my second year at REC. Open one when you want the longer version.

I finished 10th grade with 488/500 and secured 3rd place in my school, with a centum in Science.

I was also a finalist in the Way to Success examination and got the opportunity to travel to Trichy for the final stage.

At this point, I had a very different idea of where I was heading. I wanted to become a cardiologist.

I chose the Computer Science + IIT-oriented path in 11th grade.

This was not a decision I arrived at instantly. Because of circumstances and practical considerations at that stage, I had to rethink the path I had originally imagined for myself.

I scored 586/600, secured 2nd place in my school and 2nd place at the district level. I was also the only student among 700+ students to score a centum in Physics.

I finished 12th grade as the school topper with 594/600.

I scored 198 in the TNEA cutoff and 92 percentile in JEE Main. I missed JEE Advanced qualification by just one mark.

There is also a funny little memory from this phase: I was supposed to get the chance to attend a function where I could have seen Chief Minister Vijay, but my marks increased after revaluation only after the function had already ended.

I chose Rajalakshmi Engineering College for my Computer Science & Engineering degree.

I was often asked why I didn't choose CEG, MIT or SSN. One of the reasons behind my decision was simple: I could pursue all four years of my degree at REC without tuition fees, which helped reduce the financial burden on my parents.

During my first year, I explored different areas of computer science instead of deciding too quickly. Somewhere along the way, I found myself especially drawn to frontend development. I naturally tend to imagine things visually, and frontend development gave me a way to turn those ideas into something people can actually see and use.

I completed my first year with a CGPA of 9.6. I also won a Tamil quiz conducted by Yaazh Tamil Mandram, REC, and received a ₹1,000 cash prize.

My second year is where I started becoming more serious about problem solving, coding and building things.

I started my LeetCode journey and have now solved 200+ problems with 120+ active days. I also started participating more actively in hackathons.

SRM Designathon: Finalist; Top 10 among 80+ teams.

SIH Internal Hackathon 2026: Qualified through the internal hackathon and competed among 500+ teams at REC.

This chapter is still being written.
